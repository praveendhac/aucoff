3. Key decisions

Product and scope

  • Multi-tenant SaaS; operator-provisioned, invite-based tenants in v1; audited break-glass operator access; time-boxed scoped external auditor access.
  • Personas: compliance officer, auditor, engineer, executive, CISO, tenant admin, platform operator, external auditor.
  • All listed frameworks in v1 (PCI DSS v4.0.1, PCI 3DS, SOC 2, ISO 27001:2022, ISO 27701:2025, ISO 20022-1:2026 Ed.3, GDPR, DPDP, RBI DPSC and PPI, FFIEC BSA/AML 2026 and IT Handbook, FTC Safeguards, GLBA, NYDFS 500, PSD2, DORA). Framework versions are owner-supplied and must be verified.
  • Scanning is read-only; remediation is advisory. Due-diligence questionnaire agent drafts answers from real environment evidence; no auto-submission.
  • 1 year of history (stored 13 months) with any-to-any scan comparison; severity counts for the latest scan against the previous two.

UX and design

  • Light default with dark mode; professional, Gen-Z feel (rounded 8-12 px, soft shadows, large KPI numerals, gradients only on hero KPI cards).
  • Deep blue brand, teal accent; status colours with icon and text; contrast computed (text at least 4.5:1, UI at least 3:1).
  • Left sidebar, top bar with global time-range and scan selector, right detail drawer; desktop-first, tablet OK, mobile for dashboard and approvals.

Agents and AI controls

  • 13 agents: orchestrator, mapping, four scanners, document analyzer, remediation advisor, report generator, reviewer, regulatory-change, copilot, questionnaire.
  • Eval gates: citation correctness 98% or more, hallucination 1% or less, agreement 90% or more, redaction recall 99% or more.
  • Budgets: USD 25 per assessment, USD 10 per questionnaire; approvals expire after 7 days and never auto-approve.
  • Outputs leaving the tenant must be human-approved, redacted and audit-logged (AI-16).

4. Licensing and provenance rule

Owner directive: no FOSS-licence or plagiarism issue is acceptable. Only permissive licences (MIT, Apache-2.0, BSD, ISC, PostgreSQL, MPL-2.0 unmodified, OFL fonts) are allowed; if an item has an issue it is replaced. Standards text is never copied; only own summaries and clause references are stored. See constitution LIC-1 to LIC-8, NFR-15, risk R-7.
